### General Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The novel working environment brings a professional and serious atmosphere, yet it is equally friendly. The working hours are reasonably allocated, balancing work and rest, which allows me to always work at optimal productivity. Events and networking sessions are well-organized, bringing positive and vibrant feelings along with countless valuable knowledge imparted without being forced or unnatural.

**2. Support from mentors / team admins**  
The seniors always bring a friendly, positive vibe and are ready to support when needed. They actively pass on their knowledge and experience, but not in a "spoon-fed" manner; instead, they guide us patiently, laying the groundwork for us to dig deeper into the problems and find our own actual solutions. Throughout that process, they have always been patient and ready to accompany us at any time.

**3. Alignment between work and major**  
The assigned tasks and projects are completely suitable for the knowledge I learned at university. At the same time, this is also an opportunity for me to access practical experiences and new areas of knowledge. This is truly a wonderful launching pad that helps me accumulate a solid foundation for my future career.

**4. Opportunities for learning & skill development**  
During this internship, I had the opportunity to access valuable knowledge, tools, and experiences so that I can develop myself more comprehensively. This knowledge also plays an important role in my future orientation, making the products I intend and desire to create become more feasible. This is a great opportunity not to be missed to interact with experienced predecessors.

**5. Culture & team spirit**  
The company culture brings a professional and serious feeling where one can fully experience the sensation of constantly pushing oneself to grow. The serious and quiet environment also helps us stay disciplined and focused on our work.

**6. Policies / benefits for interns**  
The compensation policy is generous and totally appropriate for the effort put in. The company is also always active in creating the best conditions for interns regarding time management. The events and community days are also a big plus and incredibly valuable for the knowledge they provide.

### Other Questions

- What are you **most satisfied with** during your internship?
    Experiencing a professional, serious, and highly disciplined working environment. Being exposed to the knowledge, experience, and insights from predecessors.

- What do you think the company **needs to improve** for future interns?
    The number of registered interns is too large, leading to relative difficulty in getting approved to go to the office. This can disrupt the high motivation to work, and the subsequent long series of days not being able to go to the office causes inspiration to decline rapidly.

- If introducing it to friends, would you **recommend them to intern here**? Why?
    If that friend has a need for an internship related to the cloud, this will be a great development environment for them to build a solid cloud foundation and learn countless experiences from predecessors.
