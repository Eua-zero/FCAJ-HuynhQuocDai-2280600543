### [TASK AUTOMATION WITH AMAZON Q AI ASSISTANT AND MCP](4.1-Event1/)

&emsp;**Event Name:** FCAJ Community Day: Conference Call

&emsp;**Time:** 09:00 on May 23, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp;**Role in the Event:** Attendee

&emsp;**Brief description of the content and main activities during the event:**

- Participated in in-depth sharing sessions on applying the Amazon Q AI assistant and the Model Context Protocol (MCP) into corporate workflow automation.
- Learned about transitioning from a Single Agent architecture to a Multi-Agent architecture to solve complex problems (such as the Credit Council model) through parallel processing and cross-validation.
- Explored the operational mechanisms of MCP to connect AI with external applications (Jira, Gmail, Teams) and Context Engineering techniques to optimize context for LLMs.
- Gained access to "Enterprise-grade AI" design mindsets, including calculating the return on investment (ROI), establishing security Guardrails, and managing risks (Audit & Compliance).
- Witnessed firsthand production-grade live demos of Amazon Q's automated workflows and deconstructed the credit assessment architecture for Startups.

&emsp;**Results or value achieved:** 

- **Technical Knowledge:** Clearly understood the core operational formula of an Agent (Agent = LLM + Action/MCP) and how to build specialized Multi-Agent systems. Mastered Context Engineering methods (extracting business essentials instead of cramming raw data).
- **Development Mindset:** Realized the clear gap between theory and practical AI deployment in corporate environments. Deeply internalized the principle of solving problems through ROI ("Numbers speak louder than words") and the rule that humans are always the ultimate gatekeepers holding legal responsibility.
- **Practical Application and Soft Skills:** Shifted my mindset from "merely writing code" to designing safe, reliable automated solutions. Recognized the vital importance of foundational Software Engineering, how to grasp stakeholders' KPIs (Business, Security), and the direction to apply Amazon Q/MCP to optimize personal workflows.


### [FCAJ COMMUNITY DAY - AI RISEN & BUILDING LARGE-SCALE VOICE AGENTS](4.2-Event2/)

&emsp;**Event Name:** FCAJ Community Day: Data Driven, AI Risen

&emsp;**Time:** 09:00 on June 27, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp;**Role in the Event:** Attendee

&emsp;**Brief description of the content and main activities during the event:**

- Participated in in-depth sharing sessions regarding technology displacement and practical operational solutions in enterprises through Multi-Agent systems, DevOps AI Agents, and HR workflow automation using Amazon Q.
- Studied the decoupled architecture (STT -> LLM -> TTS) for Vietnamese Voice AI, combined with continuous Streaming mechanisms to optimize latency, control data "hallucinations," and integrate Tool Calling.
- Explored empathetic social context processing (Customer-Centric) methods for Voice Agents, such as inferring gender for standard addressing, recognizing speech pauses/interruptions, and utilizing regional accents.
- Approached enterprise-grade operational and security standards: establishing "Human-in-the-loop" safety mechanisms and a "Security-First" architecture (deploying the MCP Server within a Private Subnet via a VPC Connection).
- Observed live, real-world demos: a Voice Agent handling customer consultation responses and a DevOps Agent analyzing and proposing Root Cause mitigation plans during a live DDoS attack.

&emsp;**Results or value achieved:**

- **Technical Knowledge:** Mastered the STT-LLM-TTS pipeline structure for Voice AI and how to integrate function calls (Tool Calling). Thoroughly understood secure internal connection methods when an LLM triggers external tools via the Model Context Protocol (MCP).
- **Development Mindset:** Transformed my system design mindset from "running after technology" to a "Customer-Centric" approach (prioritizing user comfort) and evaluating success through tangible metrics (ROI). Imbued the "Human-in-the-loop" principle – humans always make the final approval decisions.
- **Practical Application and Soft Skills:** Acquired the capability and direction to independently deploy low-latency Vietnamese Voice bots; learned how to utilize Amazon Q or equivalent AI platforms to automate CV screening and system log troubleshooting; and committed to tightening VPC security audits for all API connections established from the LLM to external environments.
